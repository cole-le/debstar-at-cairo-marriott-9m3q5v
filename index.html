The idea

One book, one scan, one list that lasts.

A big event is the best week a hotel has, and the week it learns the least. The delegates belong to the organiser, the organiser keeps a spreadsheet, and the brands in the lobby go home with business cards. One shared system changes that.

Before they land

One LifeBook

Every delegate receives the event's LifeBook with the hotel's guide inside: the palace, the twelve places to eat and drink, what is open right now, and the city beyond the gates.

At the hotel

One scan

A code at check-in, in the room and on every table. The delegate gives a name, a company and what they came for, says yes to being contacted, and gets a free listing in the directory.

In the palace

Debstar on site

Bridal and event makeup and hair are what Debstar does in Lagos. For the gala night, a Debstar studio in the hotel takes bookings straight from the LifeBook.

After checkout

All year

Each company that came gets a LifeBook of its own. The hotel invites them back, the organiser sells next year's seats to people it already knows, and Debstar's shop and classes stay one tap away.

Why each side says yes

Who gains what.

The hotel

It knows who stayed, why they came and how to invite them back, and its guide sells dinner, the spa and the next event.

The organiser

A live list of every delegate and company in place of a pile of business cards. Each sponsor gets a page in the book.

Debstar Beauty Empire

A studio where the events are: gala makeup and hair, and a shop the guests take home. One hotel first, then the next.

The delegates

A free listing, a LifeBook for their company, and the people they met in Cairo, kept in one place.

Plainly

What is real today, and what is not yet.

Real today

  • The Cairo Marriott Hotel lists twelve restaurants, bars and cafés and fifteen event rooms, the largest for 806 guests, and has 1,087 rooms. marriott.com
  • Debstar Beauty Empire does bridal and event makeup, hair and wigs at its studio in Lagos. debstarbeauty.com
  • IAM360 Agency has built a website concept and LifeBook for the hotel and a proposal for Debstar.
  • The sign-up form and the free listing work today as a demo in the IAM360 Directory.

Not yet

  • No agreement with Marriott International, the hotel or its owner. None of them has seen this page.
  • No agreement with any event organiser. We name no event and no attendance figure here because we have not confirmed them.
  • No Debstar studio in any hotel.
  • Nothing here collects anyone's details. A live version asks each delegate for a yes, and follows the hotel's and the organiser's own rules on guest data.
